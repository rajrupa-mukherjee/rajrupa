Rajrupa Mukherjee

About the Artiste

Rajrupa had been in love with dance since her early childhood. To nurture her love and talent, she had initiated her Bharatnatyam training under Guru, Smt. Puspa Chatterjee who is a disciple of Guru Khagendranath Barman. Later, Rajrupa had shifted to Natanam Kalakshetra to learn under the guidance of Guru Barman and since had been his student for the past thirty years and is a regular performer in the institution’s dance festivals.

Rajrupa's first television appearance was at the age of seven in Calcutta Doordarshan where later she had also performed under the banner of Natanam Kalakshetra.

While being the dance demonstrator under her Guruji at the annual dance appreciation course- Nritya Mullayan Shikshakram, organized by the West Bengal State Music Academy, she had also been the external examiner in M. A – Bharatnatyam, (practical) at Rabindra Bharati University. A graduate from St. Xavier's College, Kolkata, Rajrupa also holds a master's degree with first class in Film Studies from Jadavpur University, Kolkata and diploma in Bharatnatyam with first division from Pracheen Kala Kendra, Chandigarh. Presently, she is pursuing her Ph. D from Jadavpur University. Rajrupa, who had been a lecturer in Film Studies/Media Studies for nine years, is also experienced in Bharatnatyam teaching in Chiranjiv Bharati School, Gurgaon and her own institution, Kaishiki in Kolkata.
